Column mapping & units

Auto safely accepts YYYY-MM-DD, YYYY/MM/DD, and YYYYMMDD. For year-last slash dates, explicitly choose MM/DD/YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY. EFL never guesses an ambiguous date interpretation.

The original byte hash and normalized-to-original row provenance are retained in the reproducibility bundle. EFL never sorts silently.
